# Final QA Checklist — Web Development

Complete before moving to `08_Launch`.

## Content Accuracy

- [ ] Business name, address, phone, hours correct on every page
- [ ] All links work (internal nav, external links, social links)
- [ ] No placeholder/lorem ipsum text remains anywhere
- [ ] No client-requested revisions from Round 1 or Round 2 are missing

## Technical

- [ ] Site tested on Chrome, Safari, and mobile browsers
- [ ] Mobile responsiveness verified on at least one real phone
- [ ] Contact form submits and email notification received
- [ ] Page load speed acceptable (under ~3 seconds on standard connection)
- [ ] SSL / HTTPS will be active at launch
- [ ] 404 page configured
- [ ] Google Analytics firing correctly (test event/pageview visible)

## SEO Basics

- [ ] Page titles unique per page
- [ ] Meta descriptions present per page
- [ ] Image alt text present
- [ ] Sitemap generated (if platform supports it)
