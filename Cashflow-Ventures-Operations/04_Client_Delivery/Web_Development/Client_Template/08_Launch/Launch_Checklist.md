## Pre-Launch

- [ ] Final QA checklist (`07_Final_QA`) fully passed

- [ ] Domain/DNS access confirmed available (or client has been sent exact DNS instructions)

- [ ] Site published to production hosting

- [ ] SSL/HTTPS active and verified
- [ ] Live site tested end-to-end (forms, links, mobile) on the real domain
- [ ] Google Analytics confirmed tracking on live domain
- [ ] Google Business Profile linked/updated with new site URL (if applicable)

- [ ] Managed Hosting & Analytics subscription ($39/mo) started — recorded in Payment Tracker as "Recurring Monthly Fee"
- [ ] Client notified the site is live, with the live URL
- [ ] Master CRM updated: Pipeline Stage → "Won" (if not already), Notes updated with launch date
- [ ] Old site (if any) backed up before decommissioning, if applicable
