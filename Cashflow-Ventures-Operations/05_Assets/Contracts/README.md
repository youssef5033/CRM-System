# Contracts

Store the standard legal/contract paperwork for both ventures here — separate from the commercial proposal templates in `02_Proposals`:

- Master Services Agreement / Terms of Service (can apply to both ventures, or one per venture)
- Any venture-specific addenda (e.g., cinematic video usage-rights terms, website ownership/IP terms)
- W-9 / tax forms or business registration documents you send to clients, if applicable

A signed proposal (from `02_Proposals/.../Accepted/`) plus a signed contract from here together form the full agreement for a project.
